# Case Study: Public Capability Proof Built in One Delivery Cycle

## Starting point

A commercially savvy, non-coding founder had strong product ideas and execution urgency, but needed public, inspectable proof of delivery capability.

## Intent

- Build a public repository that demonstrates reliable execution.
- Show clear evidence of AI-assisted collaboration without exposing private IP.
- Create reusable artefacts for employer and client trust.

## Constraints

- Tight timeline.
- Small operating team.
- Need for strict private/public boundary management.

## Outcome delivered

- Public toolkit and governance foundation shipped.
- Mission, audience positioning, and evidence framework aligned.
- Provenance and release discipline embedded into workflow.
- CI coverage and contract checks hardened for reliability signaling.

## Measurable evidence

- Multiple public commits shipped in one focused cycle.
- Branch/tag release discipline established and repeatable.
- `Table-Formatter` tests passing (29/29) across repeated validation runs.
- Public repo now contains governance, attribution, and provenance trail.

## Business impact

- Faster stakeholder trust because proof is inspectable.
- Lower rework risk through codified standards and checks.
- Stronger hiring/client narrative backed by artefacts, not claims.

## Share-safe statement

This case study reports intent and outcomes only. It intentionally excludes private methods, internal IP details, and client-sensitive context.
