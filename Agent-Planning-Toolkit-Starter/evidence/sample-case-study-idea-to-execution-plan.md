# Sample Case Study: Idea to Execution-Ready Plan

## Starting point

A non-coding but commercially savvy founder had a product idea and budget, but no clear sequence for what to build first.

## Intent

Get a practical plan that could be executed by a technical builder without weeks of rework.

## Constraints

- Time: launch window in six weeks.
- Team: founder plus one part-time technical contributor.
- Budget: moderate, with low tolerance for wasted spend.
- Risk: needed confidence before committing build hours.

## Outcome delivered

- Clear staged plan (`Now`, `Next`, `Later`).
- Prioritised first implementation checklist.
- Explicit risk and mitigation view for key decisions.

## Impact evidence

- Time-to-clarity reduced from "unknown" to one session plus one review pass.
- Decision confidence increased because trade-offs were explicit.
- Builder handoff was cleaner, with fewer clarification loops.

## Share-safe social proof line

"I went from idea overwhelm to a concrete plan I could actually act on."
