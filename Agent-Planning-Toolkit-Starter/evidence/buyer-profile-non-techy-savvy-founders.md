# Buyer Profile: Non-Techy Savvy Founders

## Snapshot

They are commercially smart, idea-rich, and execution-hungry, but do not want to become AI tooling specialists.

## Typical traits

- Strong operator instincts.
- Comfortable making investment decisions.
- Limited time for deep technical learning.
- High urgency to move from idea to traction.

## Pain points

- Too many tools and unclear starting point.
- Fear of spending money in the wrong order.
- Difficulty briefing technical builders clearly.
- Stop-start progress from overthinking architecture.

## Desired outcomes

- A clear execution path they can trust.
- Faster decisions with less cognitive load.
- A plan that can be handed to a builder with minimal translation.
- Better confidence in what to do now versus later.

## Buying triggers

- Launch pressure and missed timelines.
- New budget approved for digital or AI initiatives.
- Prior failed attempt with generic AI advice.
- Need to show leadership or investors a credible plan.

## Messaging lines that resonate

- "Bring your idea. Leave with an execution-ready plan."
- "Clarity first, before expensive build decisions."
- "Momentum without becoming an AI tools expert."
