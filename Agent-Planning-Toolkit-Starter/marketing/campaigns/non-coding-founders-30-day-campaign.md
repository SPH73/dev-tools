# 30-Day Campaign Package: Non-Coding but Commercially Savvy Founders

## Segment pain summary

- Too many AI/tool choices, no trusted sequence.
- Fear of spending on the wrong build first.
- Weak handoff quality when briefing technical implementation.

## Outcome promise

Bring your idea. Leave with an execution-ready plan you can act on with confidence.

## Trust evidence mapping

- Claim: "We turn ideas into practical execution plans."  
  Proof: `evidence/case-study-dev-tools-public-baseline.md`
- Claim: "The process is outcome-first and share-safe."  
  Proof: `evidence/case-study-employer-client-proof.md`
- Claim: "Delivery quality is repeatable, not ad-hoc."  
  Proof: root governance and CI artefacts in `dev-tools`.

## Message angles (5)

1. Clarity before complexity.
2. Decisions before build spend.
3. Faster handoff to technical implementation.
4. Proof-of-work over promise-of-work.
5. Progress without becoming an AI tools expert.

## Channel plan (30 days)

### Week 1 - Awareness

- Post: founder pain + outcome promise.
- Follow-up: one short clarity-first thought post.

### Week 2 - Proof

- Publish case-study excerpt with measurable outcomes.
- Share one pull quote and one decision-shift insight.

### Week 3 - Conversion

- Post: "What you leave with" checklist.
- CTA for a planning session or discovery call.

### Week 4 - Reinforcement

- FAQ-style post to handle objections.
- Recap thread linking proof artefacts.

## CTA variants

- "Reply `PLAN` and I will send the session format."
- "If you have idea momentum but execution fog, message me `clarity`."
- "Book a planning sprint and leave with an execution-ready sequence."

## Measurement plan

### Leading signals

- Profile views from target segment.
- Post saves and shares.
- Qualified replies using CTA keyword.

### Lagging signals

- Discovery calls booked.
- Proposal requests.
- Paid planning engagements started.

## Next iteration hypothesis

Case-study-led posts will outperform generic value posts for qualified inbound from this segment.
