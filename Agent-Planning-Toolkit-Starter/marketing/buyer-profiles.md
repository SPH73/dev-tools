# Buyer Profiles

## 1) Non-coding but commercially savvy founders

### Snapshot

Idea-rich, commercially strong, and willing to invest, but not interested in becoming AI tooling specialists.

### Core pains

- Too many tools, no clear starting sequence.
- Fear of paying for the wrong build first.
- Weak handoff quality to technical builders.

### Desired outcomes

- Execution-ready plan with clear priorities.
- Faster, lower-stress decisions.
- Better confidence in what to do now vs later.

### Messaging hooks

- "Bring your idea. Leave with an execution-ready plan."
- "Clarity first, before expensive build decisions."

## 2) Freelancers and fractional operators

### Snapshot

Service providers who must turn ambiguous client requests into practical scopes quickly.

### Core pains

- Scope creep from unclear briefs.
- Time lost in repeated clarification loops.
- Hard to standardise discovery and planning quality.

### Desired outcomes

- Repeatable intake and planning framework.
- Faster scoping with clearer client boundaries.
- Better conversion from inquiry to paid engagement.

### Messaging hooks

- "Turn vague client requests into scoped action plans."
- "Standardise quality without sounding templated."

## 3) Agency owners and boutique consultancies

### Snapshot

Small teams balancing delivery load, margin pressure, and client expectations.

### Core pains

- Senior team members stuck in early planning overhead.
- Inconsistent plan quality across team members.
- Margin loss from avoidable discovery rework.

### Desired outcomes

- Planning quality that is consistent across team.
- Faster lead-to-delivery transition.
- Stronger client trust through clearer decision artefacts.

### Messaging hooks

- "Increase planning consistency without adding headcount."
- "Protect margin by reducing pre-build ambiguity."

## 4) Internal innovation leads (SMB or mid-market)

### Snapshot

Operators tasked with introducing AI-enabled initiatives under time and accountability pressure.

### Core pains

- Pressure to deliver outcomes with limited specialist bandwidth.
- Difficulty aligning stakeholders on scope and sequence.
- Risk of tool-led projects with weak business fit.

### Desired outcomes

- Decision-ready planning artefacts for leadership alignment.
- Faster cross-functional buy-in.
- Practical rollout path with measurable milestones.

### Messaging hooks

- "Move from AI talk to accountable execution plans."
- "Align stakeholders around a shared implementation path."
