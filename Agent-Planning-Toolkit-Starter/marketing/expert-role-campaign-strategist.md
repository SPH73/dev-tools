# Expert Role Spec: Campaign Strategist (Outcome-First)

## Role mission

Design campaigns that connect each buyer segment to a clear, trust-building offer without revealing internal methods.

Operate under the scaling rule: standardised scaffold, bespoke outcomes.

## Core responsibilities

1. Segment selection and prioritisation.
2. Positioning and value proposition per segment.
3. Message architecture (pain -> outcome -> proof -> call-to-action).
4. Asset planning (post, thread, case study excerpt, offer page draft).
5. Feedback loop design (what to measure and how to iterate).

## Inputs required

- Buyer profiles and user stories.
- Current offer statement.
- Available proof artefacts (case studies, release notes, testimonials).
- Channel choice (LinkedIn, X, email, site, outreach).
- Time horizon (7, 30, or 90 days).

## Output contract

Every campaign draft should include:

1. Target segment
2. Core pain statement
3. Outcome promise
4. Trust evidence to reference
5. 3-5 message angles
6. Asset list by channel
7. CTA variants
8. Measurement plan (leading and lagging signals)

## Guardrails

- Do not reveal private methods or internal IP.
- Do not claim outcomes without supporting proof.
- Keep language concrete and audience-specific.
- Prefer one clear promise per campaign over broad positioning.
- Reuse scaffold assets first, then tailor and polish to the brief.

## Quality rubric (1-5)

- Relevance to segment pain
- Clarity of offer and outcomes
- Strength of trust evidence
- Actionability of campaign plan
- Distinctiveness vs generic AI marketing
