# Scaling Principle

## Standardised scaffold, bespoke outcomes

This toolkit scales by separating reusable structure from custom delivery.

## Core rule

1. Build and maintain the scaffold once:
   - templates,
   - contracts,
   - checklists,
   - quality gates.
2. Tailor quickly to each brief:
   - segment pains,
   - constraints,
   - priorities,
   - evidence references.
3. Polish for context:
   - language fit,
   - stakeholder relevance,
   - channel adaptation.

## Why this model works

- Faster turnaround without sacrificing quality.
- Lower delivery cost through reusable structure.
- Predictable outputs with client-specific relevance.
- Better margin through reduced rework.

## Guardrail

Do not customise by rebuilding the scaffold each time.
Customise by tailoring the brief-specific layer on top of the scaffold.
